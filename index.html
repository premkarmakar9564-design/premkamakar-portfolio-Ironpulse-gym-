Our Story

A Gym Built For This Neighbourhood

IronPulse Fitness Studio was built for the people of Garia — not for Instagram, not for corporate memberships. We wanted a gym that students, working professionals, and first-time gym-goers could walk into and feel like they belong.

We've equipped the studio with everything you need to train well: a full free weights area, quality machines, cardio equipment, and dedicated space for personal training. No gimmicks — just a serious, welcoming environment where results happen.

Whether you're here to drop weight, build strength, or simply stay active, we'll meet you where you are and help you go further.
